# Otter.ly — UNTAR Student Social Media App

**Otter.ly** (*"Otterly stay connected with others"*) is a Flutter & Dart mobile social media application inspired by Facebook and designed for the student community of **Universitas Tarumanagara (UNTAR)**.

## Key Features Implemented

- **Authentication & Onboarding:** Splash screen, onboarding carousel, student registration (Faculty, Study Program, Entry Year, NIM), login with Remember Me, forgot password, and local session storage.
- **Home & News Feed:** Campus stories, explore topic filters, trending posts, suggested communities, pull-to-refresh feed, likes, comments, share, and saved posts.
- **Posting & Media Creation:** Facebook-style post composer supporting text, single & multi-photo collage grids with fullscreen lightbox viewer, MP4 video playback, location tags, audience settings, and edit/delete post.
- **Friends & Connections:** Student directory search, faculty filters, friend suggestions, send/cancel/accept/decline friend requests, friend list management, follow/unfollow, mutual friends viewer, and block/unblock users.
- **User Profile:** Customizable cover banner & otter/gallery profile picture, student academic info card, live Friends/Followers/Following counters, My Posts & Saved Posts tabs, and account privacy controls.

## AI Usage Declaration

In accordance with academic transparency guidelines, generative AI tools were utilized in a supporting role during the development of **Otter.ly** for:

1. **Visual Assets & Illustrations:** Generating and refining mascot illustrations, onboarding artwork (`assets/images/`), default otter profile avatars, and sample campus media assets.
2. **UI/UX Ideation & Content:** Brainstorming feature categorization, sample UNTAR student demo profiles, and placeholder feed copywriting.
3. **Development & Debugging Support:** Assisting with Flutter widget boilerplate suggestions, layout troubleshooting, and documentation drafting.

All architecture decisions, feature integrations, state management, and final testing were reviewed, adjusted, and verified by the group members.
